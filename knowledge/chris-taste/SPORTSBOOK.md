## Learned rules

_Added by the design agent from feedback; the Owner keeps or undoes each one on the Studio Taste page._
- **Don’t:** Don't send players to an in-between landing page (e.g. a public shared-bet page with the bet card and an Add to betslip button) when a link is about betting. — It adds a step between the link and the bet; the sportsbook itself is the landing. _(learned 2026-11-09 from on-sportbook-after-a-bet-we-want-to-be-able-to-s)_ <!-- rule:4179a534 -->
- **Do:** Shared or deep links to a bet land on the sportsbook (/sports/<sport>) with the picks already in the betslip and the slip open, ready for a stake; logged-out players hit Join only at Place Bet. — Fewest steps from link to bet; the player lands where betting happens. _(learned 2026-11-09 from on-sportbook-after-a-bet-we-want-to-be-able-to-s)_ <!-- rule:a9a5ef57 -->
