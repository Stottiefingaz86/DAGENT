## How to use it

1. Read the BRD, name the surface rows it touches, and say them in the proposal.
2. Open the routes on the hosted site and the listed components **before** proposing. Describe what ships today in one paragraph: that is the "current state" board on the canvas.
3. Load the taste notes for those rows plus `COPY.md` and `CONFIRMATION_STATES.md`.
4. Fill SpecKit's Strategy alignment from the initiative column, with the needle and baseline from BRIEF / journey audits.
5. Run the Mobbin queries (adapt to the BRD; name a peer when the BRD does).
6. New component needed → component brief, never a one-off.
7. When a BRD lands on a surface not in this table, add a row here after Final sign-off.
8. **Before dropping a peer item because "we don't have it", check the live site** (Chris, Nov 2026: “we also do have orginals, you can see in the casino we have a carousel called orginals”). Do: check `site/casino-subnav.tsx` (`CASINO_SUBNAV`), lobby shelves in `lumen/casino-lobby-page.tsx` / `homepage-lobby-page.tsx` and `lib/lumen/casino-lobby-catalog.ts`. Don't: infer from BRIEF phasing ("in-house games = Later") that a category doesn't exist. The site ships **Originals** (`/casino/category/originals`, lobby shelf) and **New** (`/casino/category/new`).

**Every surface:** also read `POLISH.md` (celebration + sound on rewards, slight glass on chrome, house motion).
